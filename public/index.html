How it works

Ten seconds of work

Embed it in one line.

Drop the loader anywhere and it builds a responsive, lazy-loaded iframe. Or paste the iframe yourself.

Script loader

        data-video="vid_example"
        async></script>

Plain iframe

<iframe src="https://videokr.com/e/vid_example"
        allow="autoplay; fullscreen; picture-in-picture"
        allowfullscreen></iframe>

What is Videokr?

Videokr is hosted video for marketing sites. You upload an MP4 or WebM, or link an HLS, YouTube or Vimeo source; Videokr stores and delivers it and plays it in your own branded player — your logo, your colours, no vendor branding on paid plans — on any site, through an iframe or script embed. The player carries chapters, captions, CTAs, end screens and an email form, and every play is measured second by second so you can see exactly where viewers stop. It starts free forever for 500 plays a month, or $69 once — $10 off $79 — for 10,000 plays a month. More on what Videokr is (and what it isn't).

Is the free tier a trial?

No timer and no card. 5 videos and 500 plays a month, for as long as you want. Upgrade when you outgrow it, or never.

What counts as a play?

One viewer starting one video, counted once per video per month — a viewer who reloads or rewatches the same video that month is not counted again. Free stops at 500 plays until the month rolls over; paid plans keep playing and any extra plays are billed at $1 per 10,000.

Does Videokr offer a lifetime plan?

Yes — $69 once, $10 off the $79 list price, for 10,000 plays a month forever, unlimited videos, 25 GB storage and no vendor badge. The launch price steps to $99 and then $149 after the first 100 seats; if you already bought, nothing changes, ever. All lifetime sales are final and carry a 1 year warranty.

Can I host unlimited videos?

Every paid plan carries unlimited videos and unlimited embeds; storage is the only ceiling (25 GB on Starter and Lifetime, 250 GB on Agency), and uploads are up to 200 MB per file. Larger videos can live anywhere else and be linked as an MP4, WebM, HLS, YouTube or Vimeo source, and the Videokr player still plays them as your own.

Where are the videos stored, and will bandwidth bill me?

On our own global storage and delivery network, close to your viewers — or you can simply link a YouTube, Vimeo, MP4 or HLS source and keep the file where it already lives. Bandwidth is on us: there is no metered egress charge on any plan, and the free tier's monthly play allowance is the only ceiling.

Can I embed Videokr videos on my website?

Yes, on as many sites and pages as you like: one line of script that builds a responsive lazy-loaded iframe, or a plain iframe you paste yourself. Embeds can be locked to a password or a domain allow-list, and playlists embed the same way.

Does it work with WordPress?

Yes — the Videokr plugin is free on every plan, including the free tier. It adds a shortcode and a Gutenberg block that embed your hosted videos and playlists, plus an Insights screen showing your usage, plays over the last 30 days, most played videos and leads. Nothing is uploaded into WordPress, so your hosting space and bandwidth stay untouched, and plays count against your Videokr plan exactly as they do on any other site.

Am I locked in?

No. We host everything for you — no servers, no setup, nothing to maintain — and you can export your videos, leads and analytics whenever you want, so the data stays yours.
