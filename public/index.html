things people ask →

Straight answers.

Is the free tier a trial?

No timer and no card. 5 videos and 10,000 plays a month, for as long as you want. Upgrade when you outgrow it, or never.

Why no refunds?

Because the free tier lets you test every feature first, at no cost. Once the lifetime licence is issued it's yours and all sales are final.

What happens after 100 seats?

The price steps to $99, then $149. If you already bought, nothing changes — ever.

Where are the videos stored, and will bandwidth bill me?

In Cloudflare R2, which has no egress fees, or you can simply link a YouTube, Vimeo, MP4 or HLS source. Videokr is built to sit inside Cloudflare's free tier; those free quotas are generous but real, so extremely high traffic can eventually need a paid Cloudflare plan — that's a bill from Cloudflare, never from us.

Can I run it on my own infrastructure?

Yes. Lifetime includes deploying to your own Cloudflare account with your own D1 database and R2 bucket, so the data is entirely yours.
